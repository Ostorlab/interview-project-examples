**Security terms.** *Security finding*: a possible security problem, not yet proven. *Vulnerability*: a misusable weakness; searchers often mean a finding. *Owner*: who follows a finding to a decision. *Triage*: deciding which findings need attention first. *Remediation*: fixing it. *Scanner*: a tool that tests systems for findings. *Compliance certification*: an auditor's statement that a standard is met.

**Search terms.** *Impressions*: times our page appeared in search results (the SERP) for a query that month; *clicks*: click-throughs. *CTR*: clicks ÷ impressions in %; `N/A` with zero impressions, 0.00% with no clicks. *Average position*: average rank, 1 is top, 1–10 the first page. *Landing page*: `landing_url`. *Title*, *meta description*: headline and summary in results. *Intent* (S3): the searcher's goal. *Cluster* (S4): queries one page serves. *Cannibalization* (S6): our pages competing for one cluster. *Redirect*: an address forwarding to another page. *Noindex*: hidden from search; here, a draft. *H2*: section heading. *Anchor text*: clickable wording of an internal link.
