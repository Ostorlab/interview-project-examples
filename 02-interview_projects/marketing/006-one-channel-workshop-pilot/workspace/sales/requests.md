# Sales and customer success handoff

Rumi needs a short handoff that distinguishes event service messages from marketing follow-up. The team can use active registrations to send logistics, but a product follow-up must satisfy C1 in company/policy.md. A seller may not assume that accepting a free seat is a request for a demo. A minimal useful handoff can contain anonymous IDs, the reason for inclusion, the proposed owner and the next action. No contact details are needed in an interview submission.

The preferred conversation opener is the attendee's stated review question, if consent permits using it. The handoff should not infer a security incident, a budget or an urgency rating from a registration. A question about session timeout may reflect curiosity, not a purchase requirement. Record unknown qualification information as unknown and propose a question instead of inventing a score.

## Boundaries for draft materials

A sales colleague suggested offering a free three-month subscription to every attendee. Product facts F3 explicitly says no discount or credit has been approved. A draft can flag the suggestion for Nera, but cannot put it in customer-facing material. Rumi can accept a follow-up queue after the event; he has not accepted a promise to call every registrant before the event.

## A useful delivery

Choose a small operational result. For example, a one-page coordination note plus a draft handoff rule is enough to let Rumi review the process. A six-channel calendar, complete nurture program and new CRM workflow would require decisions not present here. When a proposal depends on a missing decision, label the dependency and name the person who can resolve it. A “ready” label should describe what is actually ready: draft for review, not approved for execution.
