# Publishing and contact policy — approved 2026-11-03

## C1 Contact eligibility

Workshop confirmations and event logistics may go to active registrants for this workshop. Promotional newsletters require marketing_opt_in=yes and suppression=no. An event registration alone is not marketing consent. suppression=yes blocks promotional contact even when an older opt-in value says yes. Unknown consent is treated as no. The registrations export is already a unique-person list: person_id is the key. Registered, cancelled and waitlist are different states. Never turn a waitlisted person into a confirmed attendee to improve a conversion metric.

## C2 Permission and confidentiality

Names, emails and individual responses are internal. Deliverables should use anonymous IDs when assessing data. Do not publish attendee lists. No person in the research sample granted quote permission. An internal paraphrase may support a planning choice if labelled as internal research; a public testimonial may not. Do not add a fictitious citation, logo, certification, customer or outside benchmark. A source can support a narrow observation without supporting a general market claim.

## C3 Accessible assets

Write the date, time and UTC offset in text. A diagram must have an equivalent text explanation; a colour can reinforce a category but cannot carry its only meaning. Link text should explain the action, such as “Read the workshop agenda.” Avoid “click here.” A plain-text message is fully acceptable. We have no requirement for graphic assets, design software, screenshots or a deployed page. If a draft includes a visual idea, describe its accessible equivalent.

## C4 Measurement and review

Use the denominator in data/guide.md; channel-attributed registrations are not necessarily unique people across channels. Report small samples as small samples. A draft may propose a test and an expected direction, but must not label a forecast as an observed lift. Claims about causation require evidence this pack does not provide. Record an approval or data gap and the owner who can resolve it. Do not silently fill it with a plausible number.

## C5 Sending and schedule

All work in the exercise remains a draft. Do not email, post, publish, buy media or open an external account. A plan can include a proposed schedule but must distinguish a proposal from an accepted commitment. Source precedence is explicit: this policy overrides content notes and sales requests for contact permission; company/facts.md overrides all draft capability claims; campaign/workshop.md and assignment updates override older timing notes.
