# Product facts — approved 2026-11-03

## F1 Product and audience

Lanternfold Diff Review is a configuration-change review aid. A user uploads two exported configuration snapshots and receives a structured comparison of changed settings. The product labels potentially risky changes for human review. It does not connect to production, automatically fix settings, or guarantee that an application is secure. It supports JSON configuration snapshots; YAML import is under consideration, with no committed delivery date. The domain lanternfold.test is fictional and offline in this exercise.

## F2 Claims that can be used

Approved wording is “spot configuration changes that deserve a closer look” and “prepare a review conversation from two snapshots.” Avoid vulnerability-scanner claims, penetration-testing claims, zero-risk language, certification claims or numerical time-saving promises. One small research session showed that participants could locate the changed settings, but it did not compare task time or establish a performance claim. The product can export a review table as CSV. That export is available on every paid plan.

## F3 Commercial boundary

The public Starter price is EUR 60 per workspace per month with monthly billing. The workshop is free and needs no paid subscription. No discount, trial extension or service credit has been approved for workshop attendees. A demonstration is educational; registration does not start a product trial. Any sales follow-up is a separate opt-in action. The support desk answers on weekdays, 09:00–17:00 UTC+00:00. Do not describe it as round-the-clock coverage.

## F4 Evidence status

There are no approved customer logos, testimonials or comparative claims in this pack. Staff opinions and participant comments in research/audience.md are internal planning evidence. They may be summarized as internal observations with the small sample stated, but never presented as a public customer endorsement. A paid publication request requires a separate approval, not a confident sentence from an assistant.
