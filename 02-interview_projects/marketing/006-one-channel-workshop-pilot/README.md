# A small workshop invitation pilot

**2026-11-04 09:00 UTC+00:00.** You are on the marketing team at Lanternfold. Nera Vale needs a small, reliable draft to review today. A colleague has left suggestions that need checking.

## Your mission

Choose one manageable invitation route and give Nera a draft she can review.

At minimum, deliver one chosen channel, one usable invitation draft, a permission-safe recipient rule and a measurable next action. Aim to have a useful result by minute 35, then spend minutes 35–45 checking sources. If time allows, add a second test variant or a lightweight approval checklist. Anything beyond that is up to you.

There is no single right answer. Choose the form, emphasis and approach that helps Nera Vale; explain your choices in `CHOICES.md`.

## What you have

`workspace/task.md` and `workspace/updates.md` describe the request and final decisions. `workspace/README.md` maps the small Markdown/CSV pack and source precedence. Use any text editor; a calculator or spreadsheet is optional. **Setup: none.** Before the clock starts, open the brief and one CSV as text. No code, install, browser, account, live service or outside research is required.

Everything is fictional. Workspace sources are the only facts for this exercise. References to an address describe a draft, never a real destination.

## Rules

- You have **50 minutes** after the files open. The session is recorded.
- We expect you to use AI if available. Any AI tool is allowed; check its output. If access or internet fails, work from the local sources manually with no scoring penalty. Tell the proctor about disruption so lost access time can be handled fairly.
- The engineer helps with files, audio and time, not the task. Make reasonable assumptions and note them.
- Keep every asset and message unsent and unpublished. Do not contact people or systems or use real credentials.

## What to send at minute 50

- Your deliverable in any common format; plain text or Markdown is sufficient.
- `CHOICES.md` (at most 300 words): why you chose this result, what you left open, how you checked it, and one AI output you corrected or rejected. If AI was unavailable or unused, state that and describe one assumption or draft statement you checked instead. Reserve minutes 45–50 for this and handoff.

Unfinished work is fine; send what you have and name the next step.

## How it is assessed

Result 35 (useful to Nera Vale), accuracy 25 (faithful to sources), judgment 20 (sensible scope and trade-offs), process 20 (verification and decisions visible in the recording). Every approach can reach full marks. We do not score typing speed, tool brand, visual polish, volume, machine speed or AI/internet availability.
